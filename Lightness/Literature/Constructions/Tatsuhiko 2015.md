- Adversative passives in Japanese express negative effect by an action upon a participant that is not an argument of the verb
- It can be formed from unaccusatives, unergatives as well as transitive verbs
- The affected participant has a special "fake patient" slot in the CxN which is absent in active
- According to (Shibatani 1997), the affected participant is integrated into the structure via principles of proximity (same room, whole-part, possession etc.) and affectedness (unpleasantness of action)
- There is a metonymical shift in focus from the frame of the event itself to the frame of impact on others
- Adversity implicature is supplied whenever there is a need to link a non-argument NP
- English fake resultatives (they drunk the pub dry) behave in a similar fashion
