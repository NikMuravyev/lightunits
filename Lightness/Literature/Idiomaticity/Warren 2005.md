### Key points
- **object of study:** idiomaticity in language
- **research question:** what is there in language beyond words and rules?
- **answer:** idiomaticity broadly understood as nativelike selection of idioms, which incluides the following:
	- preferences for discourse structure
	- knowledge of language-specific propostional expressions including so called formal idioms and lexicalised sentence stems
	- expressions in social interaction
	- combinatory potentials of words, including generlized meanings, argument structure, selectional restrictions
- **noteworthy observations:** 
	- collocations are not merely frequent combinations of words, they describe salient types of situations and phenomena
	- collocations are rarely noticed because they are often transparent which does not necessarily mean that that they are compositional
