### Basics

Animacy is a morphosyntactic parameter

What is animacy as a morphosyntactic parameter?
- Ontological animacy vs. grammatical animacy
- Semantic animacy vs. perspective-holding in discourse
- Animacy in gender-agreement systems, differential argument marking, differential (hierarchical) argument indexing
- Animacy hierarchy: human > animal (higher > lower) > natural force > inanimate
