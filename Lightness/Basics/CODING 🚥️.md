- No NOM, GEN and ACC case forms => ambiguity! E.g. aj iki amp woxas in principle = a boy called a dog, a dog called a boy, (someone) called boy’s dog, boy’s dog was calling, that is why possessor indexing, O-indexing and passive #form/oindexing #form/voice/passive  #role/possessor 
- In a construction, word order serves fore-/backgrounding function while case marking indicates the importance of a participant with respect to the event (which partly depends on the discourse prominence of the corresponding referent) #msyn/wordorder #infstr/focus #form/case
- Morphosyntactic coding is a trade-off between semantic frames corresponding to different verbs and argument structure constructions and processing efficiency
