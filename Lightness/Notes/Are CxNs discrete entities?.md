### Problem

Are constructions discrete entities or non-discrete clusters?

### Approaches

- [[Haspelmath 2023]] proposes that constructions are fixed templates with slots, but he aims for descriptive convenience rather than cognitive plausibility
- [[Ungerer 2023]] proposes that constructions are non-discrete
- [[Glynn & Mikkelsen 2024]] demonstrate advantages of the cluster approach based on statistical data
