## БЛАГОДАРЯ

A bright example of a grammatical shift is the Russian preposition благодаря that grammaticalized from a converb form while changing the object case. The verb благодарить ‘thank’ is transitive and takes an accusative-marked direct object, as in (1). It can be used in a temporal adverbial construction with an imperfective converb denoting simultaneity of events (2). In this case, it has a zero subject controlled by the main clause subject Петя, but its meaning and the marking of other dependents remains the same.

(1) Петя благодарит Машу за подарок.
(2) Благодаря Машу за подарок, Петя улыбнулся.

In (3), благодаря is used in its grammaticalized version as a preposition. It does not anymore denote the act of thanking but expresses the new causal meaning retaining the component ‘positive attitude’ from the original meaning, cf. (4) with a negative cause that can only be understood ironically. In this new function, it takes dative-marked complements, rather than accusative-marked, similar to вопреки, вслед, навстречу, наперекор, подобно, and согласно, cf. (5). 

(3) Благодаря Маше Петя успел на поезд.
(4) ?Благодаря Маше Петя опоздал на поезд.
(5) Навстречу Маше шел Петя.

The loss of original meaning carries along the loss of original selectional restrictions. The act of thanking requires a sentient object capable of accepting the gratitude, whereas the new function of positive cause makes this restriction redundant, cf. (6) with an inanimate dependent.

(6) Благодаря зонтику Петя не промок.

## КОМПАНИЯ

One example of a grammatical shift is the Russian relational noun компания ‘company‘ that grammaticalized into a group comitative marker as a part of a complex prepositional construction в компании NP-gen. Relational noun компания has a genitive-coded nominal valency for members of a group, normally expressed with a plural noun (1). Substituting plural for singular yields a possessor reading ‘belonging to (one’s) friend’ (2).

(1) Компания друзей шла по дороге.
(2) # Компания друга шла по дороге.

In (3), компания is used in its grammaticalized version as a comitative group modifier denoting acting together as a group. In this context компания is hosted by a more general prepositional construction of internal localization в NP-loc. The resulting complex group comitative construction в компании NP-gen expresses joint action of the subject and the secondary participant. Since the two form a minimal group of two referents, plurality of the genitive dependent is no more obligatory, cf. (4).

(3) Петя пришёл в компании друзей.
(4) Петя пришёл в компании друга.

Grammaticalization also affects the potential of a lexeme to have its own syntactic dependents. As a regular noun, кампания in the group comitative construction still combines with adjectives, as in (5), but does so relatively rarely, cf. 6,452 hits in the Russian Araneum Maximum (0.30 per million) for в Adj компании Ngen and 59198 (3.00 per million) for в компании Ngen. Interestingly, certain adjectives like тесный ‘tight’, as in (6), and уютный ‘cozy‘ describe not the group itself but the comitative relation and are thus specific to this new grammaticalized use.

(5) Ведь если вы планируете провести отпуск с семьей или _в большой компании друзей_ – это прекрасная возможность быть рядом, не лишая друг друга личного комфорта. [ARM http://www.zagranhouse.ru/article/2010/07/27/169/]
(6) Вот представьте – едете вы со смартфоном в общественном транспорте или сидите в тесной компании коллег [ARM http://www.dgl.ru/news/videoobzor-smartfona-sony-xperia-z-ot-goblina_3333.html].

After Traugott 2007: The concepts of constructional mismatch and type-shifting from the perspective of grammaticalization:
- Noun компания in the relational noun construction involves a kind of type-shifting.
- Also not only компания conforms to the semantic profile of the constructions but there is a mutual process of accomodation, as it increasingly selects “comitative“ nouns, such as сверстники, коллеги and сослуживцы.

### **Cross-Construction Comparison** 
| **Class**                | **Nominative** (`X N-GEN`/`Company of N`)        | **Locative** (`в X N-GEN`/`in the company of`/`im Kreis`)        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Animals**              | Absent                                           | **Exclusive** (pets in ru, wildlife in en)                       |
| **Friends & Companions** | Moderate (`Компания N`: comradeship)             | **Dominant** (all locatives; +coworkers/peers in `в кругу`)      |
| **Family & Relatives**   | Moderate (`Компания N`)                          | **Strong** (all locatives; extended kin in `в кругу`/`im Kreis`) |
| **Creative Groups**      | Moderate (`Company of N`: performers)            | **Strong** (`в кругу`/`im Kreis`: artists/writers)               |
| **Marginal Groups**      | Weak (`Компания N`: stars)                       | **Strong** (`в компании`/`in the company of`: criminals)         |
| **Military**             | **Strong** (`Company of N`: historical focus)    | Weak (security roles in `в кругу`/`im Kreis`)                    |
| **Age Groups**           | **Strong** (`Компания N`/`Круг N`: youth/adults) | Moderate (`в кругу`/`in the company of`: life stages)            |
| **Spiritual Figures**    | **Strong** (`Company of N`: diverse)             | Moderate (groups in `в кругу`/`im Kreis`)                        |
| **Professionals**        | **Strong** (all nominatives; formal roles)       | Moderate (+authorities in `в компании`/`in the company of`)      |
| **Abstract Concepts**    | **Strong** (`Круг N`: knowledge/rights)          | Weak (`in the company of`: philosophy)                           |

#### Key Insights:  
1. **Nominative vs. Locative**:  
   - Nominatives (`X N-GEN`, `Company of N`) favor **formal groups** (organizations, professions) and **abstract concepts** (knowledge, rights).  
   - Locatives (`в X N-GEN`, `in the company of`, `im Kreis`) emphasize **interpersonal bonds** (family/friends) and **tangible contexts** (animals, artistic circles).  
   - Exception: `Company of N` (nominative) aligns with locatives in military/spiritual themes.  

2. **Cultural Nuances**:  
   - Russian `Круг N` uniquely abstracts collectives (e.g., "покупателей" – buyers as a legal class).  
   - German `im Kreis` blends professional and emotional ties ("Geschäftspartnern" + "Lieben").  
   - English `in the company of` is the most versatile (animals to philosophers).  

3. **Semantic Shifts**:  
   - "Компания": Nominative = structured groups (companies/teams); Locative (`в компании`) = professions/roles.  
   - "Круг": Nominative = societal concepts; Locative (`в кругу`) = intimate/creative circles. 
   - "Company of": Nominative = military/historical; Locative = social/moral companionship.  

**Conclusion**: While all constructions denote "accompaniment," nominatives formalize collectives, whereas locatives personalize them. Russian/German show finer granularity in social roles, while English prioritizes contextual flexibility.
