### Background

[Mastrofini 2023] argues that the main verb in Light Verb Extensions provides aspectual information to the lexical part
### Proposal

Light verbs together with context provide viewpoint information, not lexical aspect.
- Lexical aspect of eventive nouns is already reduced compared to cognate or sinonymous verbs, cf. разрешение и разрешать/разрешить, ответ и отвечать/ответить, both nouns with a ES/directed achievement constual
- Light verbs contribute aspectual information but operate on the level of viewpoint, cf. perfective  дать and imperfective давать разрешение/ответ, do not alter telicity of the lexical noun
