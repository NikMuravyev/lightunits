A note on [[Are LVs grammaticalized?]]

Tests from [[Tetsuya 2024]] and [[Fleischhauer 2025]]:
- Adjectival/adverbial modifiers: We took a well-earned rest. (cf. * We rested well-earnedly.), cf. also neither the adjective medizinisch ‘medical’ (12a) nor the adjective erhöht ‘increased’ (12b) can be realized in a corresponding passive construction (wird ADV beobachtet).
- Aspectual meaning (boundedness): He had a walk {in / * to} the park. (cf. He walked {in / to} the park.)

How to test the degree of grammaticalization (posture verbs):

(1) LV is not a posture verb:
- Varying distance between the parts of the construction
- Varying word order of the parts of the construction
- Compatibility with motion and other postures in the context
- Modifiability of nominals with adjectives

(2) LV is not a copula:
- Agent-oriented adverbs
- Coordination or sequence with dynamic verbs
