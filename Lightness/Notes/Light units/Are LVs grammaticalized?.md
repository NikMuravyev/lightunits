### Background

Butt and Lahiri (2003), [[Butt 2010]]  “claim that light verbs are not part of the grammaticalization cline that is often posited (Hopper and Traugott 1993; see Bowern 2008 for a discussion of the state of the art with respect to this line of inquiry), but that instead light verb and full verb usages must be drawn from the same underlying lexical entry, whose lexical information plays out in different ways depending on its syntactic environment”. 
### Proposal

They instantiate weak grammaticalization, which can take varying forms and degrees.

**Verb-Verb constructions are more grammaticalized than Verb-Noun constructions**
- дай посмотрю, дал почитать > дал разрешение, дал согласие
- llevar trabajando > llevar la vida, llevar el trabajo
