## СИДЕТЬ

For illustration, consider the network of the Russian verb сидеть ‘sit’ below. Its default construction is NP-NOM V, a simple intransitive clause without dependents, e.g. Петя сидит ‘Petya is sitting‘ where сидеть is used in its most basic meaning and has a diverse set of possible grammatical forms it can be used in.

![[Pasted image 20250801122716.png]]

The immediate neighbors of NP-NOM V are (a) intransitive with a prepositional phrase NP-NOM V PP, (b) intransitive with an adverb NP-NOM Adv V, (c) intransitive with a comparative phrase NP-NOM V как XP, (d) perfectivized intransitive NP-NOM PFV-V, (e) depictive construction NP-NOM V XP-INS(dep), (f) temporal construction NP-NOM V XP-INS(temp) and (g) serial verb construction NP-NOM V V. The first three (a-c) are combinations of the verb with various syntactic constituents in which the meaning and grammatical properties of сидеть remain the same. Same is true for the perfectivized construction (d), where the verb is morphologically modified by an aspectual prefix. The only exception is the serial verb construction (f) where сидеть shows a slight shift in focus towards indicating ongoingness rather than posture.

The intransitive construction with a prepositional phrase NP-NOM V PP (a) is further subdivided into the location construction and the secondary participant construction. Location construction NP-NOM V PP(loc) denotes a spatial relation between a trajector expressed as subject and a landmark expressed with a prepositional group. This pattern gives rise to several idiomatic uses of the verb сидеть. One such use is сидеть в тюрьме ‘be in prison’ where prison is conceptualized as a both fixed and undesired location, and the verb сидеть is close in meaning to ‘be stuck‘. Another one is сидеть за работой ‘be busy with work’ where work is an abstract location that requires commitment and focus, and сидеть is thus interpreted as being busy. One more use is сидеть на диете ‘be on a diet’ which also expresses commitment but rather habitual one and stretched over a longer period of time. Secondary participant construction NP-NOM V PP(accomp) denotes the presence or absence of a secondary participant in the situation. The two idiomatic patterns arising from the use in this structure are сидеть с ребёнком ‘be babysitting‘ and сидеть без денег ‘stay without money‘. The former conveys the same meaning of commitment as сидеть за работой. The latter denotes rather a long lasting state of moneylessness. Thus different prepositional constructions highlight different relevant aspects of the sitting event while ignoring the irrelevant ones.

Another two branches, adverbial construction NP-NOM Adv V and comparative construction NP-NOM V как XP, involve the proper positional meaning and characterize the manner by means of an adverbial, as does the first construction, and a comparative phrase, as does the second one. Adverbial construction together with the prepositional pattern NP-NOM V на NP give rise to a pattern NP-NOM Adv(qual) на NP with yet another idiomatic use use of сидеть. This pattern, as e.g. in Рубашка хорошо сидит на Пете, describes a visual impression of how a certain clothing item fits a person. Here сидеть expresses a visual congruence of an object and its ground and the modifying adverb expresses the quality of this congruence, e.g. хорошо ‘well‘, отлично ‘great’. The comparative branch of сидеть provides another way of expressing the same meaning, via comparison, e.g. Рубашка сидит на Пете как влитая.

Depictive construction NP-NOM V XP-INS comes in two versions: with an adjectival depictive ADJ-INS and with a nominal one NP-INS. The nominal version involves the positional meaning whereby depictive specifies either the spatial orientation of the subject , as in Петя сидел лицом (к двери), or the manner, as in Петя сидел сычом. The adjectival version, as in Петя сидел голодным, seems to be idiomatic-only denoting a long-lasting state similar to Петя сидел без денег.

**How does the grammar of сидеть change in its idiomatic uses?**

As one moves from the center of the network towards idiomatic uses, various semantic and grammatical shifts take place. Most importantly, the core semantic component of posture becomes backgrounded and bleached to the point where it is a cancellable implicature. Thus, in (x) it is not the position of the subject ‘Masha’ that is relevant in the NP-NOM V с NP-INS but her commitment to staying and looking after the kids.

(x) Вчера Маша сидела с детьми. Всё это время она расхаживала по комнате и думала. 

This semantic shift has several consequences. First, manner adverbials that further specify the positional configuration, such as развалившись, полулёжа, на корточках, are not compatible with the idiomatic uses of сидеть, cf. (x). This is because such modifiers reinforce the bleached and irrelevant positional component of сидеть.

(x) # Вчера Маша сидела на корточках с детьми.

Second, positional sense also requires specifying location, whereas using adverbials of exact location, such as на стуле, на полу in the idiomatic uses similarly loses the idiomatic interpretation. The only exception is (y) where perceptual сидеть requires a spatial ground, Петя. In (y) there is also a shift in subject properties, as it is no longer a human participant but an inanimate one of semantic class clothes.

(x) # Вчера Маша сидела на полу с детьми.
(y) Рубашка хорошо сидит на Пете. 

Сидеть in the serial verb construction сижу курю is still available for semantic modification, as it can still combine with both position-specifying adverbials and adverbials of exact location. Yet the fact that they cannot directly follow сидеть speaks in favor of them modifying the whole complex event.

(x) Петя сидел курил развалившись / на диване.
(x) Петя сидел (* развалившись / ? на диване) курил.

Positional meaning of сидеть normally allows a viewpoint change through using the perfective counterpart сесть, as in prepositional construction in (x). As сесть expresses a motion event, the locative case of the prepositional dependent changes accordingly into the directional accusative. Same holds for idiomatic uses of the locative prepositional pattern, cf. (y).

(x) Петя сидит на стуле => Петя сел на стул.
(y) Петя сидит в тюрьме => Петя сел в тюрьму.

However, all other idiomatic uses including non-locative prepositional ones do not allow such a replacement. This speaks in favor of a separate from position semantic component of location which is maintained in all the locative prepositional patterns but is backgrounded in all other idiomatic uses of сидеть. 

(x) Петя сидел голодным => * Петя сел голодным. 
(x) Петя сидел с детьми => ? Петя сел с детьми.

**Frame-semantic description**

Сидеть in its default unmodified intransitive use is characterized by the following four frame elements: POSTURE, LOCATION, FIXEDNESS, and DURATION. POSTURE describes the spatial distribution of the object’s parts. LOCATION represents the general placement of the object with respect to a landmark. FIXEDNESS relates to the support for the object provided by the landmark. DURATION refers to the lasting state of contact between the the object and the landmark.

Each part of the grammatical network of сидеть focuses on certain frame elements while backgrounding the others. The prepositional branch introduces the landmark and thus highlights the LOCATION, FIXEDNESS, and DURATION elements while backgrounding  POSTURE. Whereas proper location constructions retain LOCATION, idiomatic uses further background it keeping only FIXEDNESS, and DURATION.

## ХВАТИТ

Another example is Russian continuative prohibitive auxilliary xватит that grammaticalized from a finite verb form while freezing in the NPST.3SG form and changing the syntactic category of dependent. The verb хватить ‘suffice’ takes a non-canonical experiencer subject and a partitive theme object in the genitive case, as in (1). It can metaphorically express sufficiency of an event, as in (2), in which case the event is represented with a verbal noun in the genitive case as well.

(1) Пете хватит денег на машину.
(2) Хватит нам на сегодня разговоров.

Sentence (3) presents a grammaticalized use of хватит as a continuative prohibitive auxilliary. Although it still relates to a continuous action болтать ‘chatter’ that has taken place up to the moment of utterance, it only vaguely indicates its sufficient quantity, but rather conveys a request not to do it any longer. In this use, хватит is synonymous with two other verbs, перестань and прекрати ‘stop’.

(3) Дети, хватит болтать! Займитесь делом.
(4) Дети, прекратите/перестаньте болтать! Займитесь делом.

As corpus example (5) shows, the sense of sufficient quantity can be absent altogether, as the event of pretending takes place only for a split second, while Sofichka acts suprised by the immediately preceding question. This is because in the imperative context the ultimate goal of the speaker is to stop any kind of ongoing action, so the temporal extent of this action is no longer relevant.

(5) А чего ему писать, когда он сам здесь? — вдруг сказал бригадир. Софичка опешила, до того голос бригадира был уверенным. Как так? — спросила она. Хватит притворяться, — сказал бригадир, — я знаю, Чунка дезертировал, и это его белье висело у тебя на веранде. [НКРЯ: Фазиль Искандер. Софичка (1997)]

## ОСТАТЬСЯ

One more example is the Russian verb остаться ‘remain’ that fuses with the caritive group без X-GEN to form a verbal caritive construction остаться без X-GEN ‘lose‘ (lit. stay without). In this context, остаться transforms into a light verb denoting a change of state. The verb остаться is an intransitive verb that takes a theme subject and either property (1) or location (2) oblique as arguments.

(1) Петя остался всё таким же дураком.
(2) Петя остался у себя дома.

Остаться can take several adverbials at once, such as a temporal adverbial на неделю ‘for a week’ and a caritive adverbial без родителей ‘without parents’ (3). In a grammaticalized verbal caritive construction shown in (4), остаться becomes part of the complex predicate of loss, limits the ability to take dependents of its own and tends to immediately precede the caritive group. Exceptions include certain adverbials, such as вообще ‘quite‘, совсем ‘altogether‘, практически ‘practically‘ that quantify the caritive object and can stand between остаться and без, as in (5).

(3) Маша осталась на неделю в городе без родителей.
(4) После аварии Маша осталась без родителей.
(5) Вася остался совсем без денег.
